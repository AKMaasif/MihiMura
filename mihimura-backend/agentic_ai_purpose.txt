Why use an Agentic AI approach instead of just a standard chatbot like ChatGPT?

The MihiMura product specification explicitly highlights this in its architecture. In agriculture, bad advice can destroy a harvest or create safety hazards. A single, standard AI model trying to guess the answer to everything from a single prompt is too risky and prone to hallucinations. 

Here is the exact purpose of using a Multi-Agent AI Swarm for this project:

### 1. Separation of Concerns & Specialization
Instead of one "god model," we break the AI into narrowly focused, specialized experts. 
*   The **Crop Vision Agent** only focuses on looking at leaf images and finding diseases.
*   The **Micro-Climate Agent** only focuses on reading weather API forecasts and assessing flood/drought risk.
*   The **Intervention & Safety Agent** only focuses on checking if a proposed chemical is safe and legal to use.
Because they are specialized, they are much easier to test, control, and update.

### 2. Tool Use and Real-World Grounding
Agents don't just "talk"; they use tools to pull real data. When a farmer asks a question, the agents actively fetch the farm's GPS coordinates, query the Sri Lanka Department of Meteorology for live weather, and check the actual crop stage in the database. The advice is grounded in real-time facts, not just what the AI learned during its training.

### 3. Safety Guardrails (The most important part)
A standard chatbot might confidently tell a farmer to apply a harsh chemical. In our agentic system, the **Orchestrator Agent** must pass every recommendation through the **Safety Agent**. If the weather agent predicts heavy rain tomorrow, the safety agent will veto the fertilizer application (because it will wash away) and suggest a delay. If the AI is unsure, the rules force it to escalate the case to a human Agricultural Instructor.

### 4. Complex Reasoning (The Agentic Loop)
When a farmer asks, "Can I apply fertilizer tomorrow?", a single chatbot might just say "Yes." But our agentic swarm performs a complex loop:
1.  **Observe**: Check the digital twin (What crop? What stage?).
2.  **Predict**: Check the weather (Will it rain?).
3.  **Reason**: Check agronomy rules (Is fertilizer needed now?).
4.  **Validate**: Run it past the safety policy.
5.  **Act**: Give the farmer a localized, safe answer.

In short, we use Agentic AI because we aren't building a chatbot; we are building an autonomous reasoning engine that treats farming decisions with the strict safety and verification they require.
